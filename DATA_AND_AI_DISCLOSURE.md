# Data and AI Disclosure Statement

## Data Sources and Preparation

This project uses publicly available data from Ma and Yang's (2026) Carnegie Endowment for International Peace study, *Who's Ahead in the Global AI Talent Race?* The data was extracted from the study's public Flourish charts, accessed on 24 October 2026.

The dataset covers 10,280 researchers from the NeurIPS 2025 cohort, with workplace information observed in 2026. The original chart data was preserved, and a processing script was used to generate the CSV files for the Streamlit application.

The sources, calculations, transformations and limitations are explained in the Data Dictionary. The extracted data is not an official individual-level Carnegie dataset, and an open-data licence for the published charts has not been verified.

## Use of Artificial Intelligence

Generative AI tools were used to assist with parts of the project, including coding, improving visualisations, checking chart designs and preparing documentation.

The specific AI-assisted tasks and their verification methods are documented in the group's AI Provenance Matrix.

## Verification and Responsibility

AI-generated suggestions were reviewed and tested rather than accepted without checking. The Streamlit application was tested locally, and differences between the Overview charts and the original visualisations were identified and corrected. The data processing script was also tested, with the regenerated CSV files matching the supplied datasets.

The findings are based on a selected sample of AI researchers and do not represent the entire global AI workforce. The data also cannot establish individual migration movements, long-term trends or the effects of government policies.
